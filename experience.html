Experience, Service, Philosophy

Experiential Summary

In 2019, I transferred Military branches from the Infantry to Simulations and attended the US Army's Simulations Operations Course. From 2021-2024, I was assigned to the Joint-Base Lewis-McCord Mission Training Complex. In this capacity, I oversaw a wide variety of Military simulations ranging from virtual enablers to large-scale constructive simulations. In addition, I supervised a small team of system administrators, network administrators and cybersecurity managers. In the Fall of 2024, I was accepted into the Advanced Civil Schooling Program and began full-time school at the University of Central Florida, where I have focused my studies on simulation visualization, and games for training. Upon graduation, I will be working as a Simulation Officer for the US Army Future Training Directorate (FTD) in Orlando Florida.

Service

Outside of my Military and academic responsibilities, I have pursued a wide variety of IT certificiations to broaden my knowledge and skills. In 2022, I earned the Certified Modeling and Simulation Professional (CMSP) certification through the National Training and Simulation Association (NTSA), and have remained active in the community. In addition, I earned the Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) certification through the International Information System Security Certification Consortium (ISC)² in August 2025. In addition, I continue to maintain, develop and refine a large library of my own personal Github coding projects to ensure that my programming abilities remain current and in-line with modern industry tools and frameworks.

Philosophy of M&S Statement

Simulations are powerful tools for training and education, providing safe and controlled environments to explore complex scenarios and develop critical thinking skills. As a Simulation Officer, I am committed to leveraging these technologies to enhance the readiness and effectiveness of our Military personnel, while leaning forward to explore future technologies through experimentation.
