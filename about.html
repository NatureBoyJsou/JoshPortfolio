Long Bio

Joshua Sourbeer is an experienced defense professional, with over 12 cumulative years of work in the field. Currently based in Orlando Florida, Joshua is pursuing a graduate degree in modeling and simulations from the University of Central Florida in the competitive advanced civil schooling (ACS) program. Joshua’s skillset is diverse; his interests include computer programming, risk management frameworks, network infrastructure, and secure software deployment. Some of Joshua’s certifications include the Certified Information System Security Professional (CISSP), Certified Modeling and Simulation Professional (CMSP), COMPTIA Security Plus and Network Plus. His awards include three defense commendation awards (ARCOM), one superior unit award, and four achievement medals (AAM), and three consecutive academic all-conference awards at the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division III level. Joshua has collaborated with a wide variety of organizations on critical defense projects, including the field testing and deployment of the Microsoft IVAS headset for Military use, and developmental consulting work where he assisted Bohemia Interactive and General Dynamics with the optimization and development of wide variety of Military simulation products. Most recently, Joshua served as a senior project manager for an organization in Tacoma, WA that provided simulation support to over 12,000 defense personnel per quarter. In this capacity, he supervised a team of 9 technical instructors, networkers, and computer programmers.
